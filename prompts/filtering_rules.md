# Expert-defined semantic filtering rules

Images are removed if any rule is clearly satisfied. If multiple removal conditions apply, the higher-priority rule is used for rule attribution.

## Rule 1. Plant-class mismatch

Remove the image if the plant species clearly does not match the label class.

## Rule 2. Main subject is not a leaf

Remove the image if the central subject is clearly not a plant leaf and leaf symptoms are not sufficiently visible.

## Rule 3. Illustration or artificial graphic

Remove the image if it is not a real photograph, such as a drawing, diagram, computer-generated image, 3D render, or artificial plant object.

## Rule 4. Document or presentation capture

Remove the image if it is a screenshot or photo from a scientific paper, textbook, slide, printed page, or similar document source.

## Rule 5. Obstructive or excessive text

Remove the image if large text, annotations, labels, or visible watermarks interfere with visual interpretation of plant disease symptoms.
